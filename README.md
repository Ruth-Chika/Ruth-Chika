# Hi, I'm Ruth Chika 👋
**Data Analyst | Excel · SQL · Power BI**

I spent 6 years in customer service at one of Nigeria's leading banks.
I handled hundreds of customers daily, managed data in CRM systems,
and tracked patterns in service delivery without even realizing that
was data analysis. Then I decided to make it official.

I transitioned into data analytics and I have not looked back since.
Right now I am in the middle of a 120-day public portfolio challenge
where I build real projects, publish everything to GitHub, and share
the messy, honest learning process on LinkedIn and X. No filters,
no faking expertise I don't have yet.

**Tech:** Excel · Power Pivot · DAX
